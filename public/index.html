No internet needed

Everything works by text message.

From any phone. Text JOIN to start.

  1. 1Sign up

    1. You send: JOIN
    2. Reply: Welcome to Betaharah. This phone is signed up. Step 1 of 3: reply with your ZIP code (in Canada, your postal code, e.g. H3W 1A1). It sets the times of sunset.

  2. 2Location and customs

    1. You send: 08701
    2. Reply: Location set: Lakewood, NJ 08701. Sunset and nightfall are now worked out for it.
    3. Reply: Step 2 of 3: customs. … A: keep them for now. B: answer the 12 questions by text. C: do it later on the website. Reply A, B or C.

  3. 3The last period

    1. You send: 1 11/1 night
    2. Reply: Recorded: flow Sun Nov 1 night, 2026. Hefsek from Fri Nov 6. 0 to undo. …
    3. You send: DONE
    4. Reply: Setup is finished. From now on text 1 when a flow begins, 2 after the hefsek, 4 after immersion, 9 for status. …

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Carrier text rates may apply.

Do I need a smartphone?

No. Any phone that can text works.

Who can see my data?

Only the phones on your account, and anyone with your calendar link.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes, on the website. Signing up needs a +1 number.

Is it in Hebrew?

The terms can be in Hebrew script. The site is in English.
