    Overview

    You record one date. The rest is worked out.

    The calendar counts by the Hebrew date, with sunset and nightfall calculated for your location.

    • Record in a few words

      Text “period last night”, or tap the day on the website.

    • Every date in order

      The hefsek taharah, each of the seven clean days, mikvah night and the onos perishah.

    • Reminders by text

      For the hefsek, each bedikah and mikvah night. You choose which ones, and when.

    How it works

    Three steps to start.

    Setup takes a few minutes. Only your most recent period is needed.

    1. 1

      Sign up

      With your mobile number and a code we text you, or with Google.

    2. 2

      Set your location and customs

      Your ZIP code gives the times of sunset. Until you choose your customs, the stricter option of each is used.

    3. 3

      Record a period

      The dates that follow appear on your calendar, and reminders go out if you turned them on.

    By text message

    Prefer texting?

    Everything works by text, without the website. Each reply lists what you can answer next.

    • “period now” or “hefsek yesterday”
    • “status” for where you are in the count
    • STOP stops every text

    Features

    Made for how couples keep it.

    Each of these is a setting. Turn on what you want and leave the rest.

    • Text reminders, your way

      Pick which reminders each phone gets, and at what time. None are sent on Shabbos or Yom Tov.

    • One account, two phones

      Add your husband’s phone. Up to four phones share one account, each with its own reminders.

    • On the calendar you already use

      Google Calendar, or any calendar app through a link. Titles in words, or symbols only.

    • Your customs

      The wait before the hefsek, onah beinonis, vest haflagah and nightfall. Set once.

    • Hebrew or English terms

      Write the terms in English letters, or in Hebrew: הפסק טהרה.

    • Private

      Signing in with Google, or with your phone. No ads. Download or delete your data at any time.

    Questions

    Questions and answers.

    More is on the Help page.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. Betaharah is free. Your carrier’s message and data rates may apply to texts.

    Do I need to install an app?

    No. It works in the browser on any phone or computer, and by text message.

    How many past periods do I need to enter?

    Only the most recent one. Earlier dates can be added later on the Log page, and the onos that depend on them are then worked out.

    Which customs does it follow?

    The ones you choose. Each point on which customs differ is a setting. Until you save them, the stricter option of each is used.

    Does it give halachic rulings?

    No. It works out dates from the Hebrew calendar, the sunset where you live and the customs you set. It does not rule on anything.

    Who can see my dates?

    The phones on your account. There are no ads, and you can download your data or delete your account at any time. The Privacy page explains how your data is kept.
