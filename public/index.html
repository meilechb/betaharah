Betaharah · בטהרה

A mikvah calendar you update by text message

Text one digit when something happens. Betaharah works out the onot perishah, the hefsek taharah, the seven clean days and mikvah night by your customs, shows them in your own calendar, and reminds you by text.

Get started How it works

Free. For family and friends. Sign in with Google, or with a phone number.

No app to open

Record by texting a number. See the dates in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, next to everything else in your day.

Your customs

Every point on which customs differ is a setting: the days of onah beinonit, Or Zarua, how the haflagah is counted, five days or four, nightfall, and more.

Private

No advertising and no tracking. Each account is encrypted before it is stored, and you can download or delete it. Signing in with Google keeps only that account’s email and name.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google, or with your phone number. A code is texted to you; type it in.
  2. Choose your location and your customs. Review the customs with your rav.
  3. Record the most recent dates, on the website or by text.
  4. Optional: connect your calendar, turn on text reminders, and add your spouse's phone to the same account.

What it calculates

The onot perishah

Onah beinonit, veset hachodesh and veset haflagah, by day or by night as the Hebrew date and the sun require, with each reason written out.

Hefsek taharah and the seven clean days

The first day the hefsek can be done and its time before sunset, then each of the seven days, and what changes if the count is broken.

Mikvah night

The night and the time of nightfall for your location, moved past Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av, with a note when it is Shabbat or Yom Tov.

What it does not do

It does not rule on halachic questions. It keeps track of dates by the customs you choose. For stains, for anything unclear, and when a veset kavua may have been established (the calendar points it out and does not apply it), ask your rav.

Text messages

Betaharah sends a sign-in code when you ask for one, replies to the commands you text, and sends reminders only if you turn them on in your settings. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help. See the Terms and the Privacy page.
