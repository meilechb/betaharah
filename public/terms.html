A calculation aid, not a ruling

Betaharah does arithmetic on dates and on the sun times at the location you set. It keeps the record you enter, works out the count and the vesatot from the settings you confirm, and can show the result on a calendar and in a text message.

It does not pasken. A stain, a colour, a bedikah, a hefsek taharah close to sunset, and every other doubt are questions for a rav. Where the program can see that an entry needs a question, it says so. Where it cannot see the question, it still has not answered it.

Until the halachic settings have been reviewed and saved, the program uses the stricter option wherever one choice adds a day of separation. Those defaults are a starting point, not a psak. The settings should be gone through with a rav.

Some situations are not calculated at all. Among them: a veset kavua (the program may point one out and say to ask a rav; it does not apply one), hormonal contraception, the particular rules of pregnancy and nursing beyond a pause you turn on yourself, and anything about a kallah, childbirth, or a medical procedure. Those are not the only cases left to a rav. In such cases the ordinary dates on the calendar may not be the ones that apply. Follow the rav.

One household

This site is a private tool for one household, not a public service. The dashboard opens with one shared password. You are responsible for that password, for the phones you list, and for who can see the Google calendar or the phone that receives the texts.

Text messages and the calendar

Text messages are not private. The telephone company and Telnyx can read them. A calendar you connect is stored in that Google account, and anyone you share it with in Google Calendar can see the events. Discreet titles and discreet reminder wording are available in Settings for a screen or a phone that other people may see.

No warranty

The program is provided as a household aid, without a warranty. A date or a time can be wrong if an entry, a setting, or the location is wrong, if the clock or the network fails, or if sunrise and sunset do not fall in the ordinary way at that place. Check anything that matters. Do not treat the program as a rav, and do not treat it as medical advice.

The record

What is stored, and how to ask for it to be deleted, is set out in the privacy policy. Removing an entry in the dashboard marks it removed. It stays in the database until the whole record is deleted.
